## 3. Spesifikasi Tetap (Locked Look)

### Wajah & Rambut
- Rambut hitam, pendek, disisir rapi ke belakang/samping (side-swept), tidak berantakan
- Tanpa kumis tebal atau jenggot penuh — **kumis tipis saja**
- Ekspresi default: ramah, senyum natural
- Kulit sawo matang, khas Indonesia

### Outfit (Tetap di Semua Produk) — TIDAK berubah oleh update palet logo generasi kedua
- **Kaos polo warna hijau** (hijau sedang/medium green, bukan hijau tua/lumut, **bukan teal**) — dikonfirmasi ulang (15 Juli 2026) tetap hijau meski Publisher Mark studio naik ke palet generasi kedua (`#07565F`); tiga warna (hijau maskot, teal logo, teal konten) sengaja dibiarkan terpisah, lihat Creative Direction Board v0.9 Bagian 1
- **Sarung kotak-kotak (plaid) hijau** — motif kotak-kotak dua nuansa hijau, diikat khas cara pakai sarung sehari-hari
- Kaki: **tanpa alas kaki (barefoot)** sebagai default

### Fisik
- Tubuh proporsional, tidak kurus, tidak gempal

## 6. Prompt Template (Siap Pakai untuk AI Generate)

```
[SUBJECT]
Indonesian man, mid-20s to early 30s, tan skin, short neat black hair
side-swept, thin mustache (no full beard), friendly natural smile,
medium build proportions

[OUTFIT — FIXED, DO NOT CHANGE]
green polo shirt (medium green, not dark/olive, not teal),
green plaid/checkered sarong (sarung) tied traditionally at waist,
barefoot [or: wearing simple flip-flop sandals — only if pose requires outdoor context]

[STYLE]
clean cutout style, isolated on plain white/transparent background (untuk diproses
ulang dengan latar custom saat komposisi cover final),
soft natural studio lighting from front-left, high detail, photorealistic

[POSE]
sitting cross-legged, leaning slightly forward as if explaining to an audience,
both hands open in explaining gestures (not holding any product), small ring
light with phone propped on mini tripod beside him (phone facing away from
viewer, screen softly glowing with no visible app interface), warm soft
lighting suggesting a live-streaming moment

[NEGATIVE / HINDARI]
no beard, no heavy stubble, no different hair color, no different shirt color
(teal/dark green/olive), no shoes, no logo text on shirt, no visible TikTok UI
or logo, no product held in hand, no branded product labels, no exaggerated
cartoonish proportions, no different face structure between generations,
no "LIVE" text overlay
```
